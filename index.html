    HERE TO HELP

    Support & frequently asked questions

    Get started with the GOLDMINE archive and learn how to find records.

    01

    What is GOLDMINE?

    An online archive for geological maps and documents held by Geological Survey Ireland.

    02

    What can I find?

    Maps, exploration reports, borehole records, publications and other geological information.

    03

    How do I use it?

    Search by a place, topic or report number, or browse the archive folders by collection.

    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Using the archive

    How do I search for a map or report?

    Enter a place name, subject, report number or phrase into Search. Narrow your results using the collection and county menus. Put a phrase in quotation marks to search it exactly.

    What is the difference between Search and Browse?

    Search finds matching records across the catalogue. Browse lets you explore folders and collections, select files, and add them to a download list.

    Which collections are available?

    The archive includes bedrock and historical maps, mineral exploration and geotechnical reports, borehole and groundwater information, publications, marine and geophysical maps, and more.

    Can I view and download original documents?

    The live archive provides document previews and download options. This demonstration uses locally generated sample previews and does not contain the original records.

    Do I need to sign in?

    Browsing this demonstration is open. The live service may ask for an email address for certain download requests; sign-in is not connected here.

    Privacy & cookie policy

    Information about privacy settings in this demonstration.

    This replica runs entirely in your browser. Search records, preview documents and downloads are generated locally; the site does not connect to a catalogue, database or analytics service.

    The cookie preference panel saves your choice in local browser storage. It does not set tracking or analytics cookies. You can revisit your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

    ABOUT THIS REPLICA

    Site usage

    Important information about this independent demonstration.

    Demonstration only

    This project is an independent, static demonstration inspired by the public GOLDMINE website. It is not an official Geological Survey Ireland service. Sample titles, identifiers, locations, dates and file sizes are illustrative and may not correspond to actual records.

    Archive access

    No live database, external archive, account service or remote document store is connected. Downloads and previews are generated in your browser for demonstration purposes only.

    For authoritative records, use the official GOLDMINE archive.
